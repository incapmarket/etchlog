Private by design

No login. No registration. No cloud. Your data never leaves your device.

Everything you enter stays on your device, in your browser. Nobody else can see it, including us. You decide what to share: nothing leaves your device unless you send a file yourself. EtchLog is free to use.

Reports you control

Print, Snapshot and read-only Share pages let you pick exactly what goes in. Send one file and it opens on any device, with no login at the other end. Snapshot and Share can carry an optional casual PIN — a simple lock to stop a shared report being opened by accident, not strong security.

What is inside

📚

Multiple books

Each book has its own categories, currencies, budgets and notes. All Books shows them together.

💱

Multi-currency

📤

🔍

Global search

Find any entry across every book, instantly.

🌙

Hide every amount on screen with one tap, and switch to a dark theme when you prefer.

Getting started

  1. Download the file with the button above and keep it in a folder you will find again.
  2. Open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. There is nothing to install and no account to create. Use your downloaded copy rather than this web page, so your books stay on your own computer.
  3. Back up now and then from Data ▾ → Backup / Restore. Your books are saved in your browser, so clearing the browser's data erases them.

How it stays offline

EtchLog runs entirely in your browser from a single file. The only network use is an optional, read-only check for notices, such as a new release, and for the term of the day. Nothing from your books is ever sent.
