EtchLog user guide

A short guide, by task. Tap a heading to open it. · About EtchLog

EtchLog is a powerful, free single-user income and expense tracker for desktop and laptop, built to a professional standard.

Free to use. EtchLog costs nothing to use.

Private by design. No login, no registration and no cloud. Everything you enter stays on your device, in this browser. Nobody else can see it, including us.

Reports you control. Print, Snapshot and Share let you choose exactly what goes into a report. Send one file and it opens on any device, with no login at the other end.

Keep as many separate books as you need, such as personal, household, a trip, college or a business. Each has its own currency, categories and budgets, and All Books shows them together.

Your data stays in this browser on this computer. To move it or keep a copy, use Backup, which saves a JSON file, and Restore it on any other computer. Snapshot and Share files open on any device, including a phone.

EtchLog is a tracker and reporter, not an accounting package: there is no invoicing, GST or double-entry bookkeeping.

  1. Tell EtchLog your name in the bar at the top the first time. You can change the book name and owner at any time by clicking them in the header.
  2. Try the sample book if you want to look around first: it is marked SAMPLE and a banner offers “Remove sample data” when you are done. New books can also include sample data.
  3. Add your first entry in the form on the left (see “Adding entries” below).
  4. Back up now and then: Data ▾ → Backup / Restore. Your data lives only in this browser on this device.
  • Home and personal: one book for everyday money, with an annual budget.
  • Family: a book for each person or purpose (the household, the children, a parent’s expenses), and All Books for the overall picture.
  • Foreign travel: a book for each trip in the trip currency, with the Rate library to convert back to your home currency.
  • College or student: a book for the term or year, with a yearly budget.
  • Small business or freelance: a book for each business or client, the reference field for invoice numbers, and the Print report for your accountant.
  • Rental or side income: a book for each property or venture.

Create a new book for every separate purpose. Books never mix, and you can look across all of them at any time.

  • Choose Expense or Income, type the Amount, pick the Currency, the date (today by default) and a category, then click Add entry.
  • Comment is optional and appears in your reports. Reference is for an invoice number, receipt, URL — or a name that ties entries together, such as a loan or a holding.
  • Use the icons on each entry to edit, copy, send to another book, or delete. A deleted entry can be brought back with Undo in the message that appears.
  • To delete several entries at once, click Select above the list and tick them. The box in the header ticks everything shown, so search or filter first to pick a group. Then click Delete selected, or Cancel to leave select mode. Select mode is only for deleting, so the Edit, Clone and Send buttons on each row are hidden while it is on. They are kept for 7 days under This book’s settings → Clear entries → Recently cleared, where you can restore them.
  • Shortcuts: press N to jump to the Amount box, and Enter in any field of the form to add the entry. Esc closes any window, cancels an edit, or leaves Select.
  • You can keep as many books as you need — one for each person, trip, business or purpose. Each book is a separate ledger with its own categories, currencies, budgets and notes. Books appear as tabs across the top; All Books shows every book side by side.
  • + Manage creates, renames, duplicates, archives, reorders and deletes books. A deleted book is kept for 7 days and can be restored.
  • This book’s settings (the gear button) holds the name, owner, description, reporting currency, colour, the currencies used, the categories, and “Clear entries” (with a 7-day undo).
  • Every book has a reporting currency. Tick the currencies the book uses in This book’s settings; each gets its own tab and appears in the entry form. Add a currency that is not listed with Data ▾ → Manage currencies.
  • The Rate library (Data ▾ → Rate library) holds a rate per currency pair per month. Each entry is converted at the rate for its own month. If that month has no rate, the nearest month is used and the figure is marked ~.
  • Rates marked SAMPLE are placeholders: replace them with your own before relying on converted totals.
  • Add, rename, reorder and delete categories in This book’s settings. If you delete a category that entries use, EtchLog shows where they are and moves them to a category you choose.
  • Loans and investments: name those categories starting with “Capital” — for example “Capital – Loan” or “Capital – Investment” — and put the loan or holding name in Reference. Capital items are kept out of your budget’s Unbudgeted line and shown on their own line instead. Interest stays an ordinary expense.
  • Each currency tab shows that currency’s totals, entries, top expenses and category breakdown for the period you pick — a week, a month, a span, the financial year, a custom range, or All.
  • Click a category name to filter the entries to it; use the search and source boxes to narrow further.
  • Consolidated (when a book has more than one currency) converts everything into one currency you choose. ⇄ Compare sets two periods side by side. 📝 Notes is a running log for the book, with date and tags.
  • The 🔍 button searches every book at once. 👁 hides all amounts (privacy mode) and ☀︎ switches dark mode.

Budgets are for spending only. You set a yearly amount for each expense category. Income is not budgeted.

  1. Open the 📊 Budget tab and click Set up budgets.
  2. Pick the budget year: Financial (April–March, the default) or Calendar (January–December). It is one choice per book.
  3. Enter a yearly amount for each expense category, in the book’s reporting currency. The monthly budget is the yearly amount ÷ 12. “Fill from last year’s actuals” and “Copy last year” give you a start.
  4. Set the Amber and Red threshold (10% by default).
  • The tab compares the month and the year to date with the budget. Status is Green, Amber or Red, based on year to date against the pro-rata budget.
  • Unbudgeted is spending in categories you have not budgeted — a hint for where to set a budget next. Capital (not budgeted) shows your “Capital …” categories. Total spent adds everything up.
  • Click any row to see the original currencies. Change the year or the “as of” month at the top. Budget amounts are in the reporting currency, so foreign amounts drift with exchange rates; a ~ means a nearby month’s rate was used.
  • Tick Budget vs actual in Print, Snapshot or Share to include it in a report.
  • The Print, Snapshot and Share buttons sit above the figures on each tab. They all ask which currencies to include, whether to show the converted total, and what to put in: Totals, Entries (with comment and reference, oldest first), Month by month, By reference, By category and Budget vs actual.
  • Print opens your browser’s print window — choose a printer or “Save as PDF”. Every page carries a footer with the book name and period.
  • Snapshot and Share save one self-contained web page you can keep or send. You can add an optional 4-digit casual PIN — a simple lock to stop a shared report being opened by accident, not strong security.
  • Data ▾ → CSV template downloads a ready-made file for your book: it lists your categories and currencies and has example lines you can copy. Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored.
  • Import CSV reads Date, Type, Category, Amount, Currency, Comment, Ref (and optionally SourceType, Source; a row with no SourceType is labelled Imported (no source)). It checks every row first: unknown currencies are flagged, likely duplicates are warned about, and nothing is added until you confirm. On All Books or Consolidated, Import CSV, Export (CSV), CSV template and a one-book backup first ask which book you mean.
  • Export (CSV) saves the book’s entries, notes and budget-versus-actual figures. EtchLog’s own export can be imported again.
  • Source list (CSV) saves one row for every combination of source type and source in all your active books, with the book name, the number of entries and the first and last dates.
  • Data ▾ → Backup / Restore saves one book or all books as a file. Keep copies somewhere safe — clearing your browser’s data erases EtchLog data.
  • Restore checks the file first and shows what it will do. A backup of all books replaces everything. A backup of one book is added as a new book, named “Restore of …” unless you change the name, and nothing else is touched. Rows it cannot read are skipped and reported, and a restore can be undone straight afterwards.
  • Browsers allow roughly 5 MB per site. The About panel shows how much you have used, and EtchLog warns you when space is nearly full.
  • ~ beside a converted figure means the entry’s own month had no rate, so the nearest month was used. A “missing rate” warning means a currency has no rate at all — add one in the Rate library.
  • Data ▾ → Show today’s term brings back the term of the day after you have closed it.
  • When you are online, EtchLog checks for notices (such as a new release) and the term of the day. None of your data is sent, and the app works fully offline.
  • Questions, ideas or a bug? Use the contact options in the About panel.

What is EtchLog?

A powerful, free single-user income and expense tracker for desktop and laptop, built to a professional standard. It runs entirely in your browser — no server, no account, no internet needed after download. Use it for home and personal money, family expenses, foreign travel, college, freelance work, small businesses and rentals, with as many separate books as you need.

Private by design. No login, no registration and no cloud. Everything you enter stays on your device, in this browser. Nobody else can see it, including us. You decide what to share: nothing leaves your device unless you send a file yourself. EtchLog is free to use.

Reports you control. Print, Snapshot and read-only Share pages let you pick exactly what goes in. Send one file and it opens on any device, with no login at the other end.

Your data stays in this browser on this computer. To move it or keep a copy, use Backup, which saves a JSON file, and Restore it on any other computer. EtchLog is a tracker and reporter, not an accounting package.

Highlights

  • Reports you control: Print, Snapshot and read-only Share pages. Pick exactly what goes in, send one file, and it opens on any device with no login. Snapshot and Share can carry an optional casual PIN (a simple lock, not strong security)
  • Multiple books, each with its own categories, currencies, budgets and notes
  • Multi-currency entries, converted at a monthly exchange-rate library you control
  • Annual expense budgets with year-to-date status in Red, Amber or Green, plus unbudgeted and capital lines
  • CSV import (with a downloadable template), CSV export, and JSON backup and restore
  • Privacy mode, dark mode, global search across all books
  • Term of the day — a finance term shown once daily · Download terms shown

How data is stored

All data is saved in your browser's localStorage — typically up to 5MB per site. Your books and entries never leave your device. Clearing browser data will erase EtchLog data. Use Backup / Restore regularly to keep a safe copy. The storage indicator below shows current usage.

When you are online, EtchLog checks for notices (such as a new release) and the term of the day. No data from your books is sent.

Browser compatibility

Works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Requires a modern browser with localStorage support. Not tested on Internet Explorer.
